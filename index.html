Your privacy

  • We never ask for your name, email, student number or reason. Your pickup code is the only thing our team ever sees.
  • Reservations are limited to 2 of each item and 3 active pickups, so there is enough for everyone. We don’t check anything else.
  • Everyone who prepares or hands over your food sees a code and a list of items. They never see who you are.
  • Delivery is optional and only offered for some menus. You choose a drop point, and handover is contactless.
  • Everything on this page is stored only in your browser. Clearing it removes every trace.
