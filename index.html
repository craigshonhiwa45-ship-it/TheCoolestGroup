Good food shouldn’t go to waste while a student goes hungry.

Second Helpings connects businesses with leftover food to schools, so students who need a meal get one quietly and with dignity.

  1. Business reports leftovers
  2. Collection picks them up
  3. Prep builds the menu
  4. School confirms who needs meals
  5. Delivery brings discreet packs
